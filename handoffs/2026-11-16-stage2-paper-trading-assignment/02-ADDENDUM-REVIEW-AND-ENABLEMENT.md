# Package 4 Addendum — Review Standard and Enablement Gate
**Added November 16, 2026 · source: Zelator's readiness-baseline confirmation, adopted into the record the same day.**

## Three separate statuses (never merged)
- **Audit:** reported complete (Muse's code-level audit + this package's readiness annex).
- **Assignment and acceptance criteria:** founder accepted (Package 4 + the 11-case test pack).
- **Implementation and independent verification:** pending. Nothing in the first two statuses moves this one.

## Zelator's review standard (binding on the review step)
After Moose implements, Zelator reviews **the actual code and the test evidence against all 11 cases** — not a green test count, not a summary. A case passes on its artifact (journal excerpt, log line, state dump), or it does not pass. Muse's verification of actual results stands as the second, separate check, per the Package 4 flow.

## Enablement gate (separate from build acceptance)
No automatic paper trading is **enabled** until BOTH: (1) the simulator passes independent verification against the 11 cases, and (2) the founder explicitly authorizes enablement. Founder acceptance of the completed build is not enablement authorization. Automation defaults OFF (readiness finding F2) and stays OFF through every stage until that explicit word.

*Ready to build is not the same as ready to operate. Implemented does not mean enabled.*
