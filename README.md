## What this is

M&Q AI CONNECT is the proposed shared intelligence and implementation layer for the M&Q ecosystem. It would supply reusable AI capabilities, founder-approved knowledge, standard workflows, and verified integration patterns so that each independent project can become stronger without losing its own mission, audience, or data boundaries.

This repository is the layer's public documentation home: the draft founding blueprint, the capability registry, the integration registry, and the versioned capability documents.

## The independent projects it would serve

All six projects are represented in the architecture, each keeping its own data store, auth, users, and operational boundary:

- **M&Q University** — free MNQ futures education. *Knowledge is the legacy.*
- **One-Minute Page** — one-minute creative pages for local shops.
- **Plates for Hope** — community meal coordination.
- **Community Bridge** — bilingual community-resource finder (Fellsmere / Sebastian / Vero Beach, FL).
- **Family Bridge** — bilingual guide for families of detained immigrants.
- **MNQ Scalp Stacks** — trading intelligence and doctrine.

## The rules that do not bend

- Projects remain structurally independent: separate databases, permissions, users, and operational boundaries.
- Sharing happens only at the founder's layer — infrastructure, standards, design, workflows, AI assistance.
- Private user information never crosses project boundaries. The three bridge projects (Plates for Hope, Community Bridge, Family Bridge) remain fully separate in data and operation. No merged databases.
- Even anonymized or aggregated information requires an **identification-risk review** before any sharing — small groups can be re-identified, and the review is a founder decision.
- Every connection must be verified before it is called "connected." One verified integration at a time.
- Nothing enters the capability library until it has been used successfully in at least one project and reviewed under the verification standard.
- Value is measured by documented time or cost savings and operational reliability — never by income promises.
- No secrets, credentials, or private data are ever committed to this repository.

## What CONNECT cannot do

M&Q AI CONNECT has **no autonomous powers**. It cannot:

- publish anything on its own,
- spend money or use credits,
- access any project's database,
- change any project's doctrine or content, or
- execute trades.

Every action at the founder's layer happens only on the founder's explicit instruction.
